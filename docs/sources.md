# Content and photo sources

Public sources checked on **18 November 2026**. Before publishing, have the hospital confirm the contact channels, OPD schedule and permission to republish the photographs and review excerpts. These checks were research, not confirmation by phone.

## Hospital and doctor

- [Hospital’s Google Maps listing](https://maps.google.com/?cid=5387212416705130336): Royal Hospital ENT & Multispeciality, near the police station in Vigyan Nagar, Kota 324005. The hospital is listed as open 24 hours. The pin is at `25.1500128, 75.8595067`. This listing and the hospital’s [contact page](https://royalhospitalkota.com/contact-us.html) give **+91 94610 19191** for the hospital; the contact page also gives **0744 2422998**. The existing site lists WhatsApp on the reception number but its link is malformed; confirm the number is active on WhatsApp before publishing.
- [Hospital overview](https://royalhospitalkota.com/about.html) lists Dr. Imran Khan as director and ENT consultant with MBBS and MS qualifications. [Practo’s doctor profile](https://www.practo.com/kota/doctor/dr-imran-khan-ear-nose-throat-ent-specialist-1) identifies his MS specialty as ENT and lists head and neck surgery. The sources disagree on training institutions and the hospital’s history includes conflicting establishment dates; those details were left out.
- [Practo’s clinic profile](https://www.practo.com/kota/clinic/royal-hospital-ent-multispeciality-vigyan-nagar) lists ENT consultation hours daily, **10 AM–2 PM and 5:30–9 PM**. These are separate from the hospital’s 24-hour listing; the site advises patients to call ahead. The reception number is kept separate from the ENT line, as published by [ThreeBestRated](https://threebestrated.in/ent-doctors-in-kota-rj).
- [Justdial’s Dr. Khan listing](https://www.justdial.com/Kota-Rajasthan/Dr-Imran-Khan-Royal-Hospital-ENT-Multispecialty-Near-New-Police-Station-PIP-Vigyan-Nagar/9999PX744-X744-230609152028-K1D6_BZDET) and [ThreeBestRated](https://threebestrated.in/ent-doctors-in-kota-rj) both give **1-MA-33, near New Police Station, Vigyan Nagar**. Practo spells the plot number differently; the website also gives the landmark and links to the hospital's Maps pin.
- [Hospital services](https://royalhospitalkota.com/services.html) and [the homepage](https://www.royalhospitalkota.com/) describe orthopaedics, general/laparoscopic surgery and urology. The doctor’s Practo profile and ThreeBestRated describe ENT services. Consultation fees, insurers and scheme eligibility can change; the site does not promise coverage or publish a fee.
- [Ministry of Home Affairs](https://www.mha.gov.in/MHA1/Par2017/pdfs/par2023-pdfs/LS19122023/2580.pdf): national emergency number **112**.
