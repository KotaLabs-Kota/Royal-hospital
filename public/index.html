Good to Know

Before you visit.

For help choosing a clinic or planning your visit, call reception.

+91 94610 19191
How do I book an appointment?

Call +91 94610 19191 and tell reception which doctor or department you would like to see. The hospital team will confirm your appointment directly.

Can I see the ENT doctor at any time?

The hospital is listed as open 24 hours, but ENT consultations have separate hours. The listed ENT hours are 10 AM–2 PM and 5:30–9 PM daily. Call ahead to check that Dr. Khan will be available.

What should I bring to my consultation?

Bring relevant reports, prescriptions and a list of medicines you take. If you have insurance or a government health scheme card, ask reception what documents you’ll need and whether your visit is covered.

How do I find the hospital?

Look for Royal Hospital near the New Police Station in Vigyan Nagar, Kota. Get directions on Google Maps.
